How to use it

  1. Watch me — a pen shows the stroke, slowly.
  2. Trace — drag from dot 1 to 2 to 3… with your finger.
  3. Dots turn green as you reach them.
  4. Your line turns amber if you wander off, and an arrow points back to the next dot.
  5. Out of order? It tells you which dot to go to instead — nothing is erased.
  6. Only one set of dots shows at a time, so the numbers never clash.
  7. Finish them and the next set appears — lift your finger and start at 1 again.
For teachers / parents

Order matters here: dots must be hit in sequence, so the child learns correct stroke direction, not just the finished shape. Corrections are redirections, never rejections — going off-path or out of order never erases work, resets progress, or shows an error. The app simply names the next dot to aim for. Nothing is graded or timed.

How this works (data-science notes)

MediaPipe Hands finds 21 landmarks on your hand each frame. Geometric features (which fingers are extended) feed a rule-based classifier — a fast, transparent baseline. Collect samples above, then train a real KNN / Random-Forest model with train_gesture_classifier.py and compare the two. Testing on a separate recording session is what gives an honest accuracy number.
